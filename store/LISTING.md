## English (en-US)

**App name**

```
Ke Bhuya? Bangla Party Game
```

**Short description**

```
One-phone Bangla imposter party game. Everyone knows the word, except one!
```

**Full description**

```
Ke Bhuya? ("Who's the fake?") is a Bangla party game for friends and family. All you need is one phone, and no internet.

How to play
• Add 3 to 12 players and pick your topics.
• Pass the phone around. Each player presses and holds to see their secret card.
• Everyone gets the same secret word, except the imposter!
• Take turns giving one clue about the word. Too obvious and the imposter will work it out.
• Talk it over and vote for who you think the imposter is.
• A caught imposter gets a last chance: guess the word and they still win.

Features
• 200+ Bangla words in 6 topics: food, places, household, jobs, festivals, sports & games
• 1 or 2 imposters
• Easy mode: show the topic to the imposter
• Clue timer (20–60 seconds)
• Scoreboard across rounds
• Play in Bangla or English
• Fully offline, no ads, no data collection

Perfect for adda, picnics, Eid holidays, weddings and long journeys.

Two more games included:
• Raja-Mantri-Chor-Police: draw secret slips and catch the thief (4 players)
• Dumb Charades: team acting game with proverbs, actions, animals and more
```
